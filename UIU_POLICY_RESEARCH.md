## RETAKE-FEE-2024 — first repeat/retake concession

- Status: verified-indexed.

Each course receives a **50% course-fee waiver only on its first retake/repeat**. The count is per course, not a student's first ever retake. Later attempts do not receive this concession. ## Calculation and configuration contract

These are product engineering decisions, not additional university regulations:

- Persist source URL, checked date, status, effective term, cohort bounds, program scope and limitations beside every policy version.
- GPA = sum(credits × grade points) / included credits. Preserve intermediate precision; display two decimals. This display convention is not claimed as UIU transcript rounding.
- F contributes zero points and no earned credits. Include graded attempts in a stated denominator model; retake histories require the selected assumption. Exclude unresolved I/W from numeric projections with an explicit incomplete-data warning until official treatment is confirmed; never silently convert I to F by wall-clock time.
- No-retake target formula: `(target × (currentCredits + newCredits) − currentCGPA × currentCredits) / newCredits`. Inputs require a known GPA denominator; aggregate earned credits are insufficient.
- Money calculations should round at clearly documented line-item boundaries in integer paisa. Neither incidental charges nor discounts should be invented.
- Never award duplicate earned credits for the same course. Course equivalencies, transferred credits, prerequisites, exemptions and electives need explicit user/adviser records.
- Unknown old-batch fees, unspecified curriculum periods and unresolved scholarship categories must fall back to manual, labeled estimates, not verified badges.
